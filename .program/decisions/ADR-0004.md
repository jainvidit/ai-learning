# ADR-0004: The gentle streak ships as designed

**Status:** accepted (genesis, 2026-07-25) · **Items:** ROOT.2.2, ROOT.4.3 · **Trigger:** OPEN-QUESTIONS #9

## Question
Streaks were adopted by the design team as intrinsic "continuity" framing; the owner
banned competition but never mentioned streaks either way (ASSUMPTIONS.md #34 [INFERRED]).

## Readings
- **A:** gentle streak + grace day ships as designed (REQ-EL-04, REQ-DW-01).
- **B:** streaks are close enough to engagement-pressure mechanics to need owner
  confirmation first.

## Decision
**Reading A.** Build the Streak projection and its dashboard surface exactly per
REQ-EL-04: grace day, learner timezone, continuity framing, no loss-threat copy
(scenario 3 is a binding check). Logged for the owner in DECISIONS-PENDING.md.

## Reasoning
Two shards require it as specced; the design already encodes the non-competitive
constraint (punitive quotas were REJECTED; the adopted form is invitational). Removal
is a projection + one card — trivially reversible. The owner's ban was on *competition
between people*; a streak compares the learner only to their own continuity.

## If the other reading is correct
Delete the Streak projection consumer surfaces and stop computing it; the append-only
event log is unaffected.
