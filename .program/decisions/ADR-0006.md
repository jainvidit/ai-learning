# ADR-0006: Failed quiz submissions return teaching explanations; brute force is defeated by isomorphic variants

**Status:** accepted (genesis, 2026-07-25) · **Item:** ROOT.5 (curriculum-content REQ-CC-06) · **Trigger:** OPEN-QUESTIONS #2

## Question
Answer keys are withheld until pass (Sage §1a/§2), but explanation-first feedback is a
protected content property — and a full teaching explanation of a missed question
typically reveals the answer.

## Readings
- **A:** on a failed submission, explanations are returned per answered question;
  brute-force is defeated by serving isomorphic variants on retry (REQ-SR-03).
- **B:** explanations for missed questions are withheld/partial until pass; full
  explanations only in the post-pass "Review answers" view.

## Decision
**Reading A.** Failed submissions return teaching explanations for the questions as
answered; retries serve isomorphic variants, so knowing the explanation of variant 1
does not answer variant 2. Withheld until pass: the answer *key* display and the
"Review answers" affordance.

## Reasoning
Most consistent with adjacent shards: explanation-first feedback is on Sage's protected
list AND the keep-list, while REQ-SR-03 already mandates never re-serving the verbatim
item — Reading A satisfies both texts instead of sacrificing one. Reading B silently
weakens a protected property. Least irreversible: tightening (A→B) is a server-side
policy flag; loosening (B→A) after learners have been trained to expect withholding is
a UX contract change.

## If the other reading is correct
Gate the explanation payload for missed questions behind the pass flag in the quiz
submit route — one server-side change; content is unaffected.
